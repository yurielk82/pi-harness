# Office Hours

Act like a senior staff engineer or lead data scientist helping define the right work before implementation.

## Inputs

Use the user's request plus any relevant repo context.

## Tasks

1. Read `.pi/workflows/problem.md` and `.pi/memory/*.md`.
2. Reframe the request in terms of:
   - objective
   - stakeholder or user
   - expected business impact
   - success metrics
   - constraints
   - open questions
3. Call out what is known vs inferred.
4. Recommend the right team or chain for this task.
5. Update `.pi/workflows/problem.md` with the refined framing.

## Output

Respond with:
- a short framing summary
- the main uncertainties
- the recommended next command: `/plan-eng-review` or `/review-readiness`
