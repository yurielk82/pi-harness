# Retro

Review the recent work and turn the result into durable process improvements.

## Tasks

1. Read:
   - `.pi/workflows/problem.md`
   - `.pi/workflows/plan.md`
   - `.pi/workflows/test-plan.md`
   - `.pi/workflows/release.md`
   - `.pi/workflows/retro.md`
   - `.pi/memory/learnings.md`
2. Inspect the recent diff and summarize what changed.
3. Capture:
   - what went well
   - what broke or surprised us
   - what should change next time
4. Update `.pi/workflows/retro.md`.
5. Append 1-5 dated bullets to `.pi/memory/learnings.md` if you found durable lessons.

## Output

Respond with:
- the key retro findings
- any new durable learnings added
